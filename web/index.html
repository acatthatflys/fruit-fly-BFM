    brain firing — who is spiking?

    Groups: R1-R6 retina, STMD small-target, VPN visual projection, DN left/right steering, pitch up/down, speed, trigger. When full MaleCNS is loaded, per-type rates appear. Bars = Hz, color = L vs R. No brain field? Run --blue brain to record.

    fly stick & throttle — what the brain is doing

    Stick X = roll (turn_gain * (R−L + vis_gain*STMD_R−L)), Y = pull (pitch_up−down). Throttle lever = throttle_bias + gain*speed. Trigger = DNp10/DNp03 rate > thresh. Fly cartoon manipulates stick based on actual DN output.
